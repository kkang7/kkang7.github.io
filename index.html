About

I am Kai Kang, an assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at UNC Wilmington. My recent research focuses on developing statistical methods for biological data.

Projects

Deconvolution

I develop deconvolution methods for RNA sequencing data. CDSeq2 estimates cell-type-specific gene expression for each individual and helps prioritize estimates for follow-up based on how reliably they can be recovered. Read the CDSeq2 preprint.

Spatial Transcriptomics

I develop statistical methods for analyzing spatial transcriptomics data.

AI Frameworks for Biological Data

I develop AI frameworks for modeling and interpreting biological data.
